The user rejected the generic geometric comparison sheets, font/label swaps and generated mascot/handcrafted-sticker concepts. They liked the candidate words but found the images soulless or generic AI art. Do not treat any existing preview as approved final identity.

The current preview studies name/subtitle hierarchy and editable typography.
The implementation uses the confirmed wording and a temporary plain P monogram
to replace the former V favicon/mark. This is not an artistically approved final
symbol. A final symbol, palette and visual system still require visual selection.
Keep the established published game and player-data identifiers compatible.
